I am currently an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Jaehyung Kim at the Machine and Language Learning Lab (ML3). I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Sogang University.

My research focuses on improving the capabilities and reliability of large language models, particularly through test-time scaling, knowledge distillation, response verification, and decoding.
