I am a research student at the Machine and Language Learning Lab (ML3), Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Jaehyung Kim. My research focuses on improving the capabilities and reliability of large language models through inference-time and post-training methods.

I am particularly interested in test-time scaling, knowledge distillation, response verification, and decoding.
