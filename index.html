About

I am a research student at the Machine and Language Learning Lab (ML3) at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Jaehyung Kim. My recent research explores how language models can make better use of their own predictions and internal signals at inference time and during post-training.

I am particularly interested in test-time scaling, self- and knowledge-distillation, reliable response selection, and efficient decoding methods for language models.
